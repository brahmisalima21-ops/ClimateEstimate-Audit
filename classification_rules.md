# Classification rules

**D1 Direct:** a recognized IAS 36 impairment or IFRS 9 ECL amount/outcome is explicitly measured and the accounting framework is confirmed.

**D2 Accounting-linked:** an explicit accounting measurement outcome or test is substantively discussed, but recognition/framework confirmation is incomplete. Merely naming IFRS 9 or IAS 36 does not qualify.

**D3 Proxy/indicator:** credit-risk or financial indicators such as PD, LGD, EAD, NPL, LLP, Z-score, loan quality, capital resilience, bank performance, financial stability, or impairment proxies. These are not automatically recognized ECL/IAS 36 amounts.

**D4 Context:** conceptual, disclosure-only, illustrative, simulation/scenario, or institutional context without direct recognized accounting outcome.

Easy mistakes to avoid: PD is not ECL; LGD/EAD alone are not ECL; NPL is not ECL; LLP is not automatically IFRS 9 ECL; an impairment proxy is not automatically IAS 36; mentioning a standard is not proof of recognition; scenario outputs are not observed recognized amounts.

The classifier is strict on purpose, but it only screens. Someone still has to check the source and apply the rules.

Workflow: identified -> title/abstract screened -> pending full text -> full text checked -> included/excluded. Record search queries, databases, dates, filters, hit counts and exports separately. Without proper search and screening records, do not report PRISMA counts.
